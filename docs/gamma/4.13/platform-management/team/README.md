# Team

The **Team** section of the Platform Management module holds the **Users**, **Groups**, and **Roles** pages.

* [**Manage users**](../manage-users.md). Add users and service accounts, accept pending registrations, assign their roles and group memberships, and manage their personal access tokens.
* [**Manage groups**](../manage-groups.md). Create, edit, and delete groups, add and remove their members, and require a group on every application.
* [**Manage roles**](../manage-roles.md). Create, edit, and delete custom roles, and manage the members of an organization role.
