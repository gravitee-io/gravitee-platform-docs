# Team

The **Team** section of the Platform Management module holds the **Users**, **Groups**, and **Roles** pages.

* [**Manage users**](../manage-users.md). Add and delete users and service accounts, assign their roles, and manage their group memberships and personal access tokens.
* [**Manage groups**](../manage-groups.md). Create, edit, and delete groups, and add and remove their members.
