# System & Security

The **System & Security** group of the **Organization** section holds the **Authentication**, **Management & Schedulers**, **CORS**, **SMTP**, **Templates**, and **Audit** pages.

* [**Configure console authentication**](../../configure-console-authentication.md). Show or hide the local login form, and add, edit, activate, and delete identity providers.
* [**Configure console management and schedulers**](../../configure-console-management-and-schedulers.md). Configure the management settings and set the scheduler intervals.
* [**Configure CORS for the Management API**](../../configure-console-cors.md). Set the allowed origins, methods, and headers, and the preflight cache duration.
* [**Configure the SMTP mail server**](../../configure-smtp.md). Turn emailing on, point the organization at your mail server, and brand the emails per recipient domain.
* [**Review organization and environment audit logs**](../../review-audit-logs.md). Read, filter, inspect, and export the audit trail of the organization or of one environment.
