# Organization

The **Organization** section of the Platform Management module groups its pages into **Assets**, **Design**, and **System & Security**.

* [**Assets**](assets/README.md). Holds the **Tenants** and **Entrypoints & Sharding Tags** pages.
* [**Design**](design/README.md). Holds the **Policy Studio** page.
* [**System & Security**](system-and-security/README.md). Holds the **Authentication**, **Management & Schedulers**, **CORS**, **SMTP**, **Templates**, and **Audit** pages.
