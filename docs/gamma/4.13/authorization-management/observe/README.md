# Observability

The **Observability** group of the Authorization Management sidebar holds the **Dashboards** and **Decisions** pages. Both read the decision records that your gateways report, and show the decisions made for the APIs your role can read.

* [**Review authorization decisions**](review-authorization-decisions.md). Find a decision, see why the PDP permitted or forbade it, and check what the gateway enforced.
* [**Monitor authorization with dashboards**](monitor-authorization-with-dashboards.md). Read the authorization overview dashboard, and build your own dashboards on decision metrics.
* [**Audit authorization changes**](audit-authorization-changes.md). Trace who created, changed, deployed, or deleted policies, entities, PDP gateways, and schema documents.
* [**Decision reporting**](decision-reporting.md). Learn which component reports each decision, where the records are stored, and what your gateways need for the pages to show data.

The tiles on the **Quick Start** page count what's configured in the environment, not decisions.
