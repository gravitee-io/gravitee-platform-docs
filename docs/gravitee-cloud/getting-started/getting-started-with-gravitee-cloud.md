# Getting started with Gravitee Cloud

To get started with Gravitee Cloud, complete the following steps

**Step 1** - Go to [Gravitee Cloud](https://cloud.gravitee.io), and then click **Sign up**. Fill in the **Create your account** form, select the checkbox to agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and then click **Register**. To sign up with your Google or GitHub account instead, click its button under **Or register with**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/cloud-sign-up-page.png" alt="The Create your account form with the First Name, Last Name, Work Email, Password, and Confirm Password fields, the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy checkbox, the Register button, and the Google, Github, and Company SSO buttons under Or register with."><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2** - If you signed up with the form, check your inbox for the confirmation email, and then click the link in it to confirm your email address. Confirm your email address before you sign in, and then [sign in to Gravitee Cloud](sign-in-to-gravitee-cloud.md).

{% hint style="warning" %}
If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder.
{% endhint %}

**Step 3** - Name your cloud account

**Step 4** - Choose _14 days Enterprise trial_ as the deployment option. The trial is your first stepping stone into Gravitee Cloud.<br>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/cloud-enterprise-trial.png" alt="The account set-up page with a company name entered and the 14 Days Enterprise Cloud Trial deployment option selected."><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 5** - Select the data residency you want. Gravitee allows you to select the region where you would like your data to reside for API Management.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/gravitee-data-residency-cloud.png" alt="The API Management Data Residency step, offering United States and Europe as the home region."><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 6** - Click on _"Get Going"_

You are then directed to the Gravitee Cloud Dashboard, where you can discover your options in Gravitee Cloud UI or go straight into API Management Control Plane by accessing APIM Console or Dev Portal.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/gravitee-cloud-default-dashboard.png" alt="The Gravitee Cloud dashboard after sign-up, showing a single trial environment and the days remaining on the enterprise trial."><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

For more information about the **Environments** section of the **Dashboard**, see [Access your environments](access-your-environments.md).
